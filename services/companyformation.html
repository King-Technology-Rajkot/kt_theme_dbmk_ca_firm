Company Formation / ROC Matters

At D B M K & Co Chartered Accountants, we specialize in providing seamless and efficient Company/LLP Formation services to help our clients establish their businesses with confidence. Our comprehensive solutions ensure that every aspect of the formation process is handled professionally and in compliance with applicable laws. Here's how we can assist you:

  1. Tailored Business Setup Consultation
    • Guidance on choosing the right business structure (Private Limited Company, Limited Liability Partnership, etc.).
    • Comparative analysis of Company and LLP structures based on taxation, compliance, and operational flexibility.
  2. End-to-End Incorporation Services
    • Preparation and filing of incorporation documents with the Registrar of Companies (RoC).
    • Assistance in obtaining Director Identification Number (DIN) and Digital Signature Certificate (DSC).
    • Name approval application through the RUN or SPICe+ portal.
  3. Drafting Foundational Documents
    • Preparation of Memorandum of Association (MoA) and Articles of Association (AoA) for companies.
    • Drafting of LLP Agreement tailored to your business needs.
  4. Registration for Tax and Other Compliance
    • GST Registration.
    • PAN and TAN application for the business entity.
    • MSME/Udyam Registration (if applicable).
  5. Banking and Post-Incorporation Compliance
    • Assistance in opening a business bank account.
    • Advisory on compliance requirements, including statutory filings and annual returns.
    • Obtaining necessary licenses or approvals specific to the business activity.
  6. Advisory on Capital Structure
    • Guidance on authorized and paid-up capital requirements for companies.
    • Structuring of partners’ contributions in LLPs.
  7. Conversion Services
    • Assistance in converting an existing partnership into an LLP.
    • Guidance on transitioning from LLP to a Private Limited Company (or vice versa).
  8. Legal and Regulatory Compliance
    • Ensuring compliance with the Companies Act, 2013, and the LLP Act, 2008.
    • Advisory on timelines and procedures to avoid penalties and delays.
  9. Ongoing Support
    • Periodic compliance reminders and updates.
    • Support for alterations in the business structure, including changes in directors, partners, or capital.
  10. Why Choose Us?
    • Experienced professionals with expertise in corporate and LLP laws.
    • Transparent and client-centric approach.
    • Timely and hassle-free process with regular updates.

At D B M K & Co Chartered Accountants, our goal is to simplify the formation process so you can focus on your business vision.
