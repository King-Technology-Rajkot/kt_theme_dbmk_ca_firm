ABOUT US

D B M K & CO is a distinguished Chartered Accountancy firm, established in 2017 through the strategic merger of four renowned firms: Jimmy Modi & Associates, Dharwal & Associates, Kharadi & Associates, and R. B. Baser & Associates. This unification brought together decades of expertise, creating a strong foundation for delivering exceptional services in Audit & Assurance, Taxation, Consultancy, and Business Advisory Services.

Since its inception, DBMK & Co. has built a stellar reputation for providing knowledge-driven legal and financial solutions in critical areas, including Goods and Services Tax (GST), Income Tax, Service Tax, Companies Act, and LLP Act. The firm partners with a diverse clientele, ranging from small businesses, MSMEs, and SMEs to large-scale enterprises and multinational corporations, offering tailored and practical solutions that drive growth and compliance.

Headquartered in Navrangpura, Ahmedabad, DBMK & Co. has an expanded presence in Patan, Himatnagar, and Vadali, ensuring that its services are accessible to a broad spectrum of clients across regions.
